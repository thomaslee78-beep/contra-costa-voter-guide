Standardized due-diligence checklist

Use the same questions for everyone: (1) What powers does this office actually have? (2) What concrete public actions or votes show performance? (3) Who are large campaign donors and independent spenders? (4) Have audits, lawsuits, ethical complaints or court findings been independently substantiated? (5) What were the responses and ultimate outcomes? (6) What will budget commitments cost over five and ten years, and what is the sunset/review mechanism?

Campaign money & political advertising

Research workspace for disclosed donors, committee funding and expenditures. No financial totals are prefilled unless independently verified. Records you enter or import are saved in this browser only. A donation, by itself, does not establish improper influence.

Reading the money trail: Direct contributions go to a candidate or ballot-measure committee. Independent expenditures are made separately to support or oppose a choice. Ad expenditures are a subset of spending, not additional money to add to a committee's total spending. Organizations and their employees are distinct donors. Industry tags below are researcher-assigned, not official government classifications.

Official research portals: CA state Power Search · FPPC top-ten donors · Contra Costa local disclosures · FEC federal disclosures. School district and city races may have their own filing officers.
