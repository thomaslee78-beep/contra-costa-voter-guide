Standardized due-diligence checklist

Use the same questions for everyone: (1) What powers does this office actually have? (2) What concrete public actions or votes show performance? (3) Who are large campaign donors and independent spenders? (4) Have audits, lawsuits, ethical complaints or court findings been independently substantiated? (5) What were the responses and ultimate outcomes? (6) What will budget commitments cost over five and ten years, and what is the sunset/review mechanism?
